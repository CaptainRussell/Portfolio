Financial Readiness · AFAS 3410

The lesson

Lesson objectives and samples of behavior

Cognitive lesson objectiveUnderstand the significant elements of military pay, allowances, and the Blended Retirement System.

Affective lesson objectiveRespond to the importance of understanding financial fitness.

  • Cite the importance of understanding the Blended Retirement System. Pick a TSP contribution and see the match you keep or leave behind, then what it is worth after 20 years. The pension, continuation pay, Roth vs Traditional, and why you zoom out when the market drops. Chapter 3: The BRSChapter 1: 68% of my net worth is the TSP
  • Identify the Three Types of Pay. Base pay (taxed), allowances such as BAH and BAS (not taxed), and special and incentive pay, found on a real LES and then on your own first one. Chapter 2: Your first LESChapter 1: Your discussion post
  • React to various money management scenarios. Five calls from a lieutenant's first ten years: the TSP, a car, a surprise bill, marriage and a promotion. Then my own mistakes and what I got right. Chapter 4: Money callsChapter 1: What I got wrong

Before class: the Military Pay Compensation discussion post (a single O-1 with 0 years, off base in San Antonio, 2026 rates). Chapter 1 starts with your answer.
