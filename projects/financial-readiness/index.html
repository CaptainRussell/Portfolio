Financial Readiness

An animated, interactive lesson for cadets about to become second lieutenants: military pay, allowances, the Blended Retirement System, and my real path from −$20,000.

The idea

New lieutenants get the biggest pay raise of their lives on the day they commission, and most of them have never been taught what to do with it. So I use my own numbers, from a broke college grad with student loans in 2019 to a net worth near $300,000 today. It's a lot easier to buy in when it's a real person's numbers and not a made-up example.

Cadets start with the pay they worked out before class, follow my net worth year by year, build their own first Leave and Earnings Statement from the three types of pay, pick a TSP contribution under the BRS and see what it is worth in 20 years, then make five money calls from a lieutenant's first ten years and compare their line with mine.

Built on five books

  • The Richest Man in Babylon (George S. Clason): pay yourself first.
  • The Millionaire Next Door (Thomas J. Stanley and William D. Danko): most millionaires don't look like millionaires.
  • Millionaire Mission (Brian Preston): do things in the right order.
  • Money for Couples (Ramit Sethi): actually talk about money with your spouse.
  • The Simple Path to Wealth (JL Collins): spend less than you earn, invest the rest in low cost index funds.

The numbers are my own and the projections are illustrations at 7% a year after inflation. Nothing here is financial advice.
